CGPA to percentage formulas

Universities in India do not all use the same method. The most widely used formula comes from CBSE, but several universities publish their own. Always use the one printed on your grade card if it is available.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert CGPA to percentage?

For a 10-point CGPA, multiply by 9.5 (the CBSE method). Some universities use their own formulas, such as VTU and Mumbai University, which are included in the calculator above.

How do I convert percentage to CGPA?

Divide your percentage by 9.5. For example, 76% ÷ 9.5 gives a CGPA of 8.0.

How do I convert an Indian percentage to a 4.0 GPA?

There is no single official formula. Many applicants use grade bands like the table above. Universities and credential evaluators may assess your marks differently, so check their guidance.

Which formula should I use for my university?

Use the formula printed on your grade card or published by your university’s examination office. If neither exists, request an official conversion letter.

Is this result accepted for admissions and visas?

Treat it as an estimate. Admission offices and visa processes rely on official transcripts and, where required, an evaluation report.
